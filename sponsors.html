Sponsors

Your support puts real tools in students' hands and sends NC State to the national stage. Let's build the future of wind energy together.

Why sponsor Windpack

Invest in hands-on engineers

Windpack is a fully student-led team competing in the Collegiate Wind Competition. Sponsorship directly funds the materials, tooling, and travel that turn classroom theory into a working wind turbine, and connects your organization with motivated students entering the clean-energy workforce.

  • Brand visibility - your logo on our turbine, apparel, banner & website
  • Recruiting access - meet driven engineering & development talent
  • Real impact - power a student team representing NC State nationally
Where it goes

How your support helps

Every contribution - financial or in-kind - goes straight into the team.

Materials & Fabrication

Composites, 3D-printing filament, electronics, and the raw materials behind every prototype and revision.

Tools & Equipment

Test rigs, sensors, and shop equipment that let the team build and validate the turbine in-house.

Travel to Nationals

Registration, transport, and lodging to get the whole team to the national competition each year.
