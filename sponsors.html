Why sponsor Windpack

What sponsors get

Windpack is run entirely by NC State undergraduates. Sponsorship pays for what it takes to build a turbine and bring it to the competition, and it puts your company in front of engineering students who will be looking for internships and jobs.

  • Your logo on our turbine, team apparel, competition poster, and this website
  • Our member resume book and time with the team at recruiting events
  • A team that placed 6th of 10 in its first season, and is building its second turbine now

Where the money goes

Nobody on the team is paid, so money and donated parts go straight to the turbine and the trip to the competition.

Materials & fabrication

ABS filament for the blades, steel and aluminum for the tower, and the motors and electronics inside the turbine.

Tools & equipment

Sensors, test gear, and shop tools so we can build and test the turbine ourselves.

Travel to the competition

Transport and hotel rooms to get the team and the turbine to the regional event each spring.

Sponsorship levels

Any level can be met with money or with donated parts, software, or services. The sponsor packet has the details, or email us and we will work out something that fits.
