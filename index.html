Who we are

Windpack is NC State's student team in the Collegiate Wind Competition.

Each year the competition asks college teams to build a working wind turbine from scratch and make the business case for where a real one should go. We compete in both halves: our engineers design and build the turbine, and our Project Development team studies real sites, analyzes wind data, and presents a full project plan.
