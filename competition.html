The Collegiate Wind Competition

College teams each build a model wind turbine and plan a full-size wind farm, then present both to industry judges.

What it is

Two contests

The Collegiate Wind Competition is run by the non-profit REpowering Schools. For 2027 it goes by CWC+, the Clean Energy Workforce Competition. Every team enters both of its contests.

In Turbine Design and Testing, each team builds a turbine that fits inside a 45 cm cube, writes a design report of up to 15 pages, and tests the turbine in a wind tunnel for power output, safe shutdown, and durability. During the year, teams also send in two videos: first the generator making controlled power, then the whole turbine doing it in wind.

In Project Development and Stakeholder Engagement, each team plans a wind farm inside an area the organizers choose, adds other clean energy on the same land to power a hypothetical large energy user, works out the permits and the costs, and runs at least two outreach events with industry and the local community.

In 2026 the competition moved from one national event to three regional events, each hosted by a school with its own wind tunnel.
