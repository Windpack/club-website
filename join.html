Get involved

Join the Pack

All majors, all experience levels. If you're curious about wind energy, hands-on engineering, or project development — there's a spot for you.

Who can join

No experience required — just show up

We welcome students from every college and year. Pick the team that excites you, or try a few until something clicks.

Like building things?

The Mechanical team is for you — CAD, fabrication, blades, and drivetrain.

Into electronics & code?

The Electrical team handles power electronics, controls, and data.

Strategy, data & outreach?

Project Development covers siting, analysis, the business case, and community.
