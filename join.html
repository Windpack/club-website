Pick your team

Three teams, one entry. You tell us which one you want on the interest form.

Mechanical

Build the part that spins.

Blades, hub, pitch system, tower. Last season that meant 3D-printed ABS blades, a bevel-gear pitch mechanism, and a TIG-welded steel tower.

  • Aero & Blade
  • Structure
  • Pitch

Mostly MAE and MSE majors.

Electrical

Turn spin into power.

Generator, power electronics, controls. Last season that meant an Arduino running the turbine, an MPPT controller, and a brake that trips by itself if a wire breaks.

  • Controls
  • Power & Generator

Mostly ECE and CS majors.

Project Development

Plan the whole wind farm.

Where to build it, how much wind the site gets, permitting, the grid connection, and what it all costs. No engineering required.

  • Site Assessment
  • Environmental & Permitting
  • Grid Interconnection
  • Supply Chain

Any major.

How to get in

  1. 1

    Sign the interest form

    Six questions, under a minute, open all year. It is how we reach you about everything else.

  2. 2

    Apply when the window opens

    Twice a year, at the start of the fall and spring semesters. The interest list gets the link before anyone else.

    Next: start of spring 2027
  3. 3

    Meet your sub-team

    In your first week you are in our Slack and Notion and sitting down with your sub-team head. Then you get to work.

The deal

The honest version, so nobody is surprised later.

Any major. Zero experience.

Nobody shows up already knowing how to build a wind turbine. You do not need a wind or engineering background to apply.

Undergrads only.

Collegiate Wind Competition rules do not let graduate students work on the entry. Not our call.

Show up.

Go to your sub-team's meetings. Times are on the Windpack calendar. Miss three without telling your lead and your spot goes to the next person on the interest list.

Spots open mid-semester.

Schedules change. When a spot opens between windows, the team lead fills it from the interest list first. A member recommended you? Great. Fill out the same form.

One minute. Six questions.

Prefer a new tab? Open the form directly. Questions go to windpack-org@ncsu.edu.
